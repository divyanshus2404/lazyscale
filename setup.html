1

Forwarding gives us a copy of the enquiry, not access to your mailbox. We cannot read anything else, and replies go out from our address with you copied — so you always see the thread.

Stopping is one step

Change the form action back, or delete the forwarding rule. That is it — nothing to cancel with us, no account to close, and we lose access the moment you do it.

Replies are off until you ask for them. To begin with we only forward and summarise; nothing is ever sent to your customer on your behalf. When you have read a week of them and want it to start replying to the straightforward ones, tell us and we will turn it on — for strong enquiries first.
