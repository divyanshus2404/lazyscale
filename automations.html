Before we quote anything

We score every request against four questions. Two failures and we will tell you it is not worth building, or suggest a smaller version that is.

  1. Does it happen weekly or more? Automating a monthly twenty-minute job saves four hours a year.
  2. Is the saving countable in hours or rupees? If nobody can name a number, nobody will value it in month three.
  3. Can a person approve before anything irreversible happens?
  4. Do you already own the tools? If it needs a migration first, that is a different and larger project.

When we will tell you not to automate

Some of the best calls we have end with us talking someone out of a project.

  • It happens rarely. The build costs more than it saves.
  • The process is still changing. You would be paying us to rebuild it every month. Wait until it settles.
  • The real problem is upstream. If the leads are bad, automating follow-up just processes junk faster.
  • One person does it and likes it. Automation that removes the interesting part of someone’s job gets quietly worked around.
