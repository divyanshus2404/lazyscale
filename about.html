About

There is one person behind this.

My name is Divyanshu Singh. I build the automations, I connect them to your tools, and when one breaks at 2am it is my phone that goes off. There is no team behind me and no support queue in front of me. The person you talk to is the person doing the work.

LazyScale exists because most businesses here don't lose customers on price. They lose them because someone was asleep, or on a call, or the message landed on WhatsApp while everyone was watching email. The enquiry was fine. The timing wasn't.

What I am selling is response time. Everything I build points at that one number.

Sold as employees, not software

Each AI employee arrives with a written job description, a probation period and a monthly performance review. That sounds like a gimmick until you have been sold software that does forty things and none of them well. A job description says what a thing will do, and more usefully what it will not.

What I won't build

Seven things, published in full next to the fifty-five I will. Nothing that moves money on its own, nothing that pretends to be a person, no cold outbound at scale, no scraping behind a login. The list is on the catalogue page, with the reason under each one.

What I can't claim yet

No case studies, no testimonials and no customer count, because there are none. When there are, they will appear with real numbers attached. Until then the page built to publish response times sits empty rather than filled with something plausible.

The whole thing is public on GitHub, including the endpoints that do the work and the tests that keep them honest.
