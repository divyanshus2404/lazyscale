Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 August 2026

LazyScale is a small automation studio. We collect the details you type into our forms so we can reply to you, and nothing more. We do not sell your data, and we do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Who we are

LazyScale answers customer enquiries for small businesses in India. For anything relating to this policy or your data, contact divyanshus2404@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you choose to give us, plus the standard technical information any website receives.

We do not ask for payment card details, identity documents, or any special category data on this website. Please don't send them to us through these forms.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies, and there is nothing here to consent to. We store one item in your browser's local storage, your light or dark theme preference, so the site looks the same when you return. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it.

We use Vercel Web Analytics to see which pages get visited and which buttons get used. It is cookieless, does not follow you across sites, and does not collect anything that identifies you personally. See how it handles data.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for 24 months from your last contact with us, so we have context if you come back. If you become a client, we keep records for as long as our engagement lasts and for any period we're required to afterwards. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and comparable laws elsewhere, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data of yours we hold
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete
  • Delete your data
  • Withdraw the consent you gave when you submitted a form
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf

Email divyanshus2404@gmail.com and we'll action it within 30 days. There's no charge. If you're unhappy with how we've handled a request, you can raise it with the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are encrypted in transit. We keep the number of people and systems that can reach your data as small as we practically can. That said, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is meant for people running or working at businesses. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle data you've already given us, we'll email you where we reasonably can.
