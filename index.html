What We Build

Two things we do better than anyone

We could automate anything. We've chosen to be excellent at the two that make startups money — speed of reply, and the channel your customers actually use.

01

Speed-to-Lead

Every enquiry answered in under 60 seconds — whatever time it lands.

Leads arrive scattered across your website, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, IndiaMART and someone's personal phone. We pull them into one pipeline, qualify and score each one against your criteria, alert your team, and send a genuine first reply before the lead has finished comparing you to a competitor.

  • One inbox for every channel your leads use
  • Scored and prioritised, so you call the right person first
  • Instant personalised reply, with your booking link
  • Nothing sits unanswered overnight or over a weekend

Why this first: replying in under five minutes rather than thirty transforms how many leads convert. It is the most reliably profitable automation we build.

02

WhatsApp Autopilot

Your business, running properly on the channel India actually uses.

Most automation agencies treat WhatsApp as an afterthought because they were built for markets where nobody uses it. Your customers message you there and expect an answer there. We handle enquiries, order and delivery updates, appointment reminders, payment nudges and the same fifteen questions you answer every single day.

  • Capture and reply to leads from Click-to-WhatsApp ads
  • Order, booking and delivery updates sent automatically
  • Routine questions deflected, real ones escalated to a human
  • Official WhatsApp Business API — not a browser hack that gets you banned

Why this matters: your customers already live here. Being fast and organised on WhatsApp is a bigger advantage in India than anything you can do on email.
