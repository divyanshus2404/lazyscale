How we did it

  • The same two questions to every one, asking as a customer would. Nobody was told it was a test.
  • A reply means a person answering the question. An automatic “we’ll get back to you” that led nowhere is counted as no reply, and noted.
  • We are not naming any business. The point is the pattern, not a callout.
  • Twenty businesses in one area is a small sample. Treat it as a sniff test, not a statistic.
