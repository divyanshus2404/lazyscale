# Messages to send

Ready to send, on WhatsApp, to people who already know you. Change the first few
words and send. Do not attach anything and do not send a link in the first
message — a link in a first message from someone you know reads as a forward.

**Send five tonight.** Expect three to ignore you. That is the normal result, not
a verdict.

---

## The rules these follow

1. **No pitch.** One sentence on what it does, one offer, one question.
2. **The question at the end does the work.** A message ending in a full stop is
   easy to leave on read; a message ending in a question is awkward to ignore.
3. **Free is the whole offer.** You have no case studies. Free is what you have
   instead, and it is worth more than a discount.
4. **Never open with "hope you're doing well".** They know it is a pitch, and it
   makes them brace.
5. **No emoji, no bold, no bullet points.** It is a message, not a brochure.

## Gym, salon, clinic, coaching centre

> Hey [name], random question — when someone DMs your Instagram asking about
> membership or a slot, how long does it usually take to get back to them?
>
> I've built something that answers those in under a minute, any time of day. I
> want three businesses to try it free for a month so I have real results to show.
> Yours would be a good fit. Want me to set it up?

**Why this one works:** it opens with a question about their business, not about
you, so the first reply is easy to give.

## Real estate, interior design, anything with big-ticket enquiries

> Hey [name], quick one. Out of the enquiries you get in a week, how many do you
> think you lose just because nobody got back to them fast enough?
>
> I've built something that replies to every enquiry in under a minute and tells
> you which ones are actually worth calling. Setting it up free for three
> businesses this month to get real numbers. Interested?

**Why:** in a high-value category, one saved deal pays for a year. Say the number
out loud on the call.

## Anyone selling on WhatsApp

> Hey [name], do you get most of your enquiries on WhatsApp now? I keep hearing
> that from everyone.
>
> I've built something that answers the routine ones instantly — hours, pricing,
> availability — and passes anything real straight to you. Free for a month for
> the first three businesses I set it up for. Want to try it?

## A friend who runs anything

> [name], I need a favour and it might also be useful to you.
>
> I've built an AI thing that answers customer enquiries in under a minute. I need
> three real businesses to run it on so I have actual results instead of claims.
> Free, no catch, and you can tell me to switch it off whenever.
>
> Can I set it up on yours this week?

**Why:** with a friend, asking for help is more honest than pitching, and it works
better.

## Someone who already said no, months later

> Hey [name], no pitch — just an update. That automation thing I mentioned is
> running properly now. If you ever want to see what it does with your enquiries,
> say the word. Otherwise ignore this entirely.

**Only send this once**, and only if it is true.

## When they reply

### "How much?"

> Free for the first month, properly free, nothing to cancel. After that ₹9,999 a
> month if it's actually helping, and nothing if it isn't. I'd rather show you than
> explain it.

### "What exactly does it do?"

> Someone messages you at 11pm asking about prices. Instead of sitting till
> morning, it replies in under a minute with a real answer, and tells you the next
> morning which enquiries are worth calling back. Anything awkward — a complaint,
> someone haggling — it doesn't touch, that comes straight to you.

### "Does it actually work?"

Do not oversell. This answer is the most important one in this document.

> Honestly, you'd be one of the first. That's why it's free — I need real results
> more than I need your money right now. If it doesn't help in a month, you've lost
> nothing and I've learned something.

### "Send me details"

> lazyscale.vercel.app/details

That page exists for exactly this. One screen, no login, opens fast on a phone.

### "Not right now"

> No problem. Can I check back in a couple of months?

Then actually write it down and actually check back.

## What to do when someone says yes

1. **Ask the baseline question first**, before anything is built:
   *"Right now, roughly how long does it take you to reply to a new enquiry?"*
   Write the answer down. That number is what sells your next ten clients.
2. Send [`ai-employees/lead-responder-jd.md`](ai-employees/lead-responder-jd.md)
   so they know exactly what it will and will not do.
3. Follow the delivery sequence in [`README.md`](README.md). Do not skip the
   shadow run.
4. At day 30, ask for the testimonial. Agree this up front, in
   [`FOUNDING-CLIENTS.md`](FOUNDING-CLIENTS.md) terms, not at the end.
