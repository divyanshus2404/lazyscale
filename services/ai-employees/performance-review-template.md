# Monthly performance review — template

Issued for every AI employee, every month, whether or not the client asks. An employee
you cannot evaluate is a black box, and black boxes get cancelled at the first budget
review.

**Send it even in a bad month.** A review that only ever reports good numbers stops
being read. The one that says "this got worse and here is why" is the one that earns
the renewal.

Keep it to one page. Nobody reads two.

## Notes for whoever writes this

- **Pull the numbers before writing the words.** Writing first invites fitting the
  story to a conclusion.
- **Never round in your own favour.** One checkable "cut first response from 4 hours
  to 90 seconds" is worth more than any percentage.
- **If a month is bad, lead with it.** Burying it costs the account when they find it.
- **Recommending a downgrade wins renewals.** The client who was told to pay less is
  the one who refers you.
- Month 1 doubles as the founding-client case study. Write it well enough to publish,
  with permission.
