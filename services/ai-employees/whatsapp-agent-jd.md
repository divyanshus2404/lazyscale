Send this before the build starts, not after. It is the scope document — it is what

---

## What this role owns

**Every WhatsApp message that arrives on your business number, from the moment it
lands to the moment a person takes over.**

Your customers already live on WhatsApp and expect an answer there. This role makes
that channel behave like a staffed desk instead of a phone somebody checks.

## What it does, in order

1. **Receives** every message on your WhatsApp Business number through your BSP.
2. **Reads** what was actually asked, including voice notes transcribed to text.
3. **Answers** anything your knowledge base covers — hours, location, pricing bands,
   delivery, policies, availability — usually within seconds.
4. **Escalates** anything it does not cover, to a named person, with the thread.
5. **Handles out of hours** honestly: an acknowledgement with a real timeframe, never
   a promise nobody will keep.
6. **Sends** order, booking and delivery updates you trigger.
7. **Logs** every conversation, and reports monthly on what got escalated and why.

## What it escalates to a person

It stops and hands over — without replying — when:

- The question is not covered by the knowledge base
- The tone is angry, or it is a complaint
- Someone wants to negotiate price or asks for a discount
- The message concerns an existing order, payment or refund
- Someone asks for a human
- The message is a voice note it could not transcribe confidently
- It cannot produce an answer it is confident in

**Escalation is correct behaviour, not failure.** A thin knowledge base produces an
agent that escalates everything, which is useless but honest. A month of repeated
escalations for the same reason is a gap to close, and we will bring it to the review.

## What it will never do

- Send anything to a broadcast list, or to numbers you did not get permission from
- Take a payment, share a payment link you did not approve, or confirm a transaction
- Negotiate, discount, or commit to a price outside the bands you gave it
- Promise a delivery date it was not given
- Speak on your behalf about anything legal, medical or financial
- Reply at all when it is unsure — it escalates instead

**On broadcast specifically:** we do not build it, at any price. Messaging people who
did not opt in gets your number banned, and it is your number, not ours. Opt-in lists
through Meta-approved templates only.

## What you need to know about WhatsApp itself

These are Meta's rules, not ours, and they shape what this role can do:

| | |
|---|---|
| **The 24-hour window** | Once a customer messages you, you can reply freely for 24 hours. After that, only a pre-approved template message can open the conversation again. This is why out-of-hours handling matters so much. |
| **Template approval** | We draft them, Meta approves them, and the timing is entirely theirs. Budget days, not hours. |
| **Your account, not ours** | The WhatsApp Business API account is yours. We connect to it. If you leave, the number and the history stay with you. |
| **Verification** | Meta verifies the business before the number goes live. This is the step most likely to delay launch, and it is outside anyone's control. |

## What we need from you

| | |
|---|---|
| **A BSP account** | Gupshup, AiSensy, Interakt or similar, in your name. We assist with setup; we do not own it. |
| **The knowledge base** | Hours, location, pricing bands, delivery, returns, the fifteen questions you answer every day. **This is the entire product.** A thin one produces a useless agent. |
| **A named manager** | One person who reads escalations. Without one, nobody notices when something drifts. |
| **Baseline numbers** | Current message volume, median reply time, and how many go unanswered overnight. Without these there is no way to show what changed. |

Budget real time for the knowledge base. It is the single input that decides whether
this works, and it is the one clients consistently underestimate.

## Onboarding

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1** | BSP access, knowledge base drafted together, baseline recorded |
| **2–3** | Built, connected, tested against at least twenty of your real past conversations |
| **4–5** | **Shadow run.** It drafts every reply, sends nothing. You read what it would have said. |
| **Meta** | Verification and template approval — timing is theirs |
| **Go live** | Answering only the categories you signed off |
| **6–30** | Coverage widened as the knowledge base proves itself |

The shadow run is not optional. It is the reason go-live is quiet.

## How performance is judged

Reviewed monthly against the baseline recorded on day one:

- Median and worst-case reply time, in hours and out of hours
- Messages handled, and the share handled without a person
- Escalations, and whether the same reason keeps recurring
- Messages that got no reply at all
- Hours returned to whoever used to watch the phone

A monthly review document is issued whether or not you ask for it.

## Probation and ending it

**30 days.** At the end, either it stays at the agreed rate, or we hand over and
switch it off. The number, the account and the conversation history are yours either
way — there is nothing to extract and nothing held hostage.

## What this role is not

It is not a chatbot with a decision tree, and it is not a replacement for whoever
handles your difficult customers. It answers the fifteen questions that do not need a
person, so the person is free for the ones that do.
