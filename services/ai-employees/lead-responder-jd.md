## What this role owns

**Every inbound enquiry, from the moment it arrives to the moment a human takes over
or the lead goes cold.**

That ownership is the whole point. Nobody on your team needs to check whether it ran,
chase it, or remember to reply. If an enquiry arrives, it is handled.

## What it does, in order

1. **Watches** your enquiry channels — website form, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, and
   `sales@`. Up to three sources included; more are quotable.
2. **Reads** what actually arrived, rather than pattern-matching a keyword.
3. **Scores** it 0–10 against the criteria we agree in the kickoff call.
4. **Replies** within 60 seconds, referencing the specific thing they asked about, with
   a booking link.
5. **Alerts** whoever is on duty, in your channel, with the score and a one-line summary.
6. **Logs** the lead and the full thread to your CRM or sheet.
7. **Follows up** twice if there is no reply, then stops and tells a human.

## What it escalates to a person

It stops and hands over — without replying — when:

- The enquiry is a complaint, or the tone is angry
- Someone asks to negotiate price or terms
- The message is about an existing order, account or invoice
- Someone explicitly asks for a human
- It scores below your auto-reply threshold
- It cannot produce a reply it is confident in

**Escalation is correct behaviour, not failure.** A month with zero escalations means
the criteria are too loose, and we will say so at the review.

## What it will never do

- Send anything to a cold or purchased list
- Negotiate, discount, or commit to a price
- Promise a delivery date it was not given
- Take payment or move money
- Speak on your behalf about anything legal, medical or financial
- Reply at all when it is unsure — it escalates instead

## What we need from you

| | |
|---|---|
| **Access** | The three channels, your CRM or sheet, and your alert channel. Into a password manager, not a chat. |
| **Criteria** | Your best three customers and your worst three enquiries, described concretely. This is the single input that decides whether it works. |
| **A named manager** | One person who reads the escalations. Without one, nobody notices when something drifts. |
| **Baseline numbers** | Your current volume, response time and miss rate, before we start. Without these there is no way to show what changed. |

Vague criteria produce a scorer that rates everything a 7. Expect us to push hard on
this during kickoff — it is not a formality.

## Onboarding

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1** | Access granted, criteria agreed, baseline recorded |
| **2** | Built and tested against at least ten of your real past enquiries |
| **3–4** | **Shadow run.** It drafts everything, sends nothing. You read what it would have said. |
| **5** | Goes live, with the auto-reply threshold set high |
| **6–30** | Threshold lowered as the scoring earns trust |

The shadow run is not optional. It is the reason go-live is quiet.

## How performance is judged

Reviewed monthly against the baseline recorded on day one:

- Median and worst-case first response time
- Enquiries handled, and the share handled without a human
- Escalations, and whether the same reason keeps recurring
- Enquiries that got no reply at all
- Hours returned to the person who used to do this

A monthly review document is issued whether or not you ask for it.

## Probation and ending it

**30 days.** At the end, either it stays at the agreed rate, or we hand over
documentation and credentials and part on good terms. No exit fee, no notice period,
no argument.

If it goes, the workflows you paid for keep running. You simply stop getting support,
tuning and monitoring from us.

## What this role is not

It is AI running on workflows we build and maintain. It is not a person, and we are
not staffing you — the contract is a service agreement, not employment.

It does not replace your salesperson. It removes the part of their job that is
copying, chasing and re-typing, so the hours go to the conversations that actually
need one.
